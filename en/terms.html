This is a translation of the Dutch terms. If the two differ, the Dutch text applies.

1. Who they apply to

These terms apply to every order placed with Motion Cosmos (Dutch Chamber of Commerce, KvK 42203622). Motion Cosmos works only for businesses and organisations, not for consumers.

2. Offer and prices

  • The prices on this website are in euros and exclude VAT. Companies in the Netherlands pay 21% VAT. For companies in another EU country with a valid VAT number the VAT is reverse-charged, and companies outside the EU pay no Dutch VAT.
  • For work that doesn't fit one of the packages, you first get a quote by email. It is valid for 30 days.

3. Ordering and payment

  • You order by email and you pay upfront. With the invoice you get a payment link, or you can transfer the amount to the account on the invoice.
  • We start the work as soon as the payment has arrived.
  • With Every month you pay each month upfront: the first month when you order, and after that at the start of each month with a payment link you get by email. You cancel it by email, and it stops from the next month.
  • We may decline an order, for example if we can't deliver the work. You then get the full amount back.

4. Delivery and revisions

  • We deliver the videos as files by email or through a download link, in the formats of the package. We agree the delivery time per order.
  • Every paid video has one revision round: you send all your changes in one go, and we change the video accordingly. More rounds or a new idea is new work, with a quote.
  • You make sure we may use the logo, images and texts that you supply or approve.

5. Using the videos

  • Once paid, you may use the videos without restriction for your own business, including in advertising.
  • We only show work with your name or logo in our portfolio or on social media if you agree to that in writing.

6. Refunds

If we can't deliver a paid order, you get back the amount paid for that part. Because we only work for businesses, there is no statutory cooling-off period.

7. Liability

Our liability is limited to the invoice amount of the order concerned, except in the case of intent or gross negligence.

8. Law and questions

Dutch law applies to every order. Questions or a complaint? Email sem@motioncosmos.com; you get an answer within five working days.
