Privacy statement

Version October 2026

This is a translation of the Dutch privacy statement. If the two differ, the Dutch text applies.

Who we are

Motion Cosmos (Dutch Chamber of Commerce, KvK 42203622) is responsible for the data below. Send questions about privacy to sem@motioncosmos.com.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and loads no fonts or scripts from other parties. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) keeps technical logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time for security.

What data and why

  • First contact. We email companies about our work, at a general email address that the company publishes on its own website. For that we keep the company name, that address and the page where it is published. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in showing companies our work. Every email includes a way to opt out.
  • Orders. For an order we use the names, email addresses and messages of the people we email with. Legal basis: performing the order.
  • Invoices and payments. Invoices show the company name, address and VAT number. Payments go through Mollie. Legal basis: legal obligation and performing the order.

How long

  • Invoices and the accounts: 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires.
  • If you opt out, we keep only the address and the opt-out, so that we don't email you again.
  • Other emails and data: until at most two years after the last contact.

Who else processes the data

Google (email, storage, Google Workspace), Mollie (payments) and GitHub Pages (this website). We don't sell or rent out data.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, and object to its use. Email sem@motioncosmos.com; you get an answer within a month. If you disagree with us, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
